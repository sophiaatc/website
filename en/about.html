About the company

Engineering oriented toward operational safety in ATS environments.

We are a genuinely Brazilian company combining expertise in technology and systems development with experience in aviation and air traffic control.

Who we are

SOPHIA ATC is formed by highly qualified professionals with expertise in both technology and systems development, as well as aviation and air traffic control. The company emerged from the need to improve situational awareness for controllers, operators, and airport administrators.

Our mission is to develop effective tools that support Air Traffic Control and airport operations, transforming data into clear, traceable, and useful information that increases the efficiency and safety of operational processes.

Design principles

Safety first

Every product evolution starts with operational impact and preserving ATS environment reliability.

Non-invasive architecture

The suite operates as an intelligence layer, preserving the primary system and avoiding unnecessary changes.

Full auditing

Interactions, events, and informational context can be preserved for investigation, analysis, and continuous improvement.

Operational usability

Interfaces and flows are designed for real work contexts, focused on clarity, speed, and low distraction.

SOPHIA APP

Integrates meteorological, operational, and regulatory data to support approach-control operations with centralized, auditable information available in real time.

SOPHIA SAMUEL

An airport situational-awareness platform that monitors vehicles and mobile assets, identifies protected-zone violations, and immediately alerts ground operators and Air Traffic Services, helping prevent runway incursions.

Our Journey

An evolution driven by operational maturity, technological integration, and modular expansion.

2025

Proposal foundation

The SOPHIA suite began development with a focus on situational awareness, operational safety, and integrating data relevant to the controller.

2026

First structured module

SOPHIA APP became the main front, combining meteorological data, alerts, and support for approach operations.

2026

SOPHIA SAMUEL

Development of the platform from air traffic control experience, technical discussions with controllers, airport operators, field teams, and vehicle drivers, together with preventive analysis of real runway-incursion occurrences.

2026

Multimodule portfolio

Expansion of the suite with solutions for approach operations and surface safety, alongside continued evolution of the TWR, ACC, FIS, and AFIS modules.
