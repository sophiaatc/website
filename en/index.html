Aviation · Airports · Air Traffic Services

Systems engineering for the safety and efficiency of aviation.

SOPHIA ATC builds systems for air traffic services, the airport environment, and aeronautical infrastructure. We integrate operational, meteorological, and normative data to expand situational awareness and strengthen air navigation safety.

Where SOPHIA ATC works.

A shared engineering foundation — cyber safety, data auditing, and non-invasive integration with existing infrastructure — applied to different layers of air operations.

Air Traffic Services

Consolidation of meteorology, charts, publications, and standards into the working environment of those who control traffic, reducing information fragmentation and the time spent searching for critical data.

  • Situational awareness
  • Integrated meteorology
  • Publications and standards
  • Operational decision support

Airport Environment

Surface monitoring and tools that support runway incursion prevention, with real-time tracking of vehicles and aircraft on the ground during landings and takeoffs.

  • Surface safety
  • Runway incursion prevention
  • Ground monitoring
  • Airport situational awareness

Infrastructure and Communications

Systems for integration, message exchange, and interoperability between aeronautical services, designed for resilience, traceability, and operational continuity.

  • Systems integration
  • Aeronautical message exchange
  • Interoperability between services
  • Architectural resilience

From manual records to evidentiary backing.

We replace scattered notes with an auditable, tamper-evident history. It becomes possible to reconstruct with precision the informational scenario available to the operations team at the moment of any occurrence.

Operational safety
Less workload and less information fragmentation
Traceability

Data integration

Incursion prevention

Applied Innovation

SOPHIA ATC development is oriented toward the coherent integration of multiple information layers in air operations, with emphasis on operational usability, traceability, and architectural resilience. New systems are in development to expand this coverage.
